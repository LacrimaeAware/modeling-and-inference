Can pulses distinguish two feedback mechanisms?

A cell can slow the production of mRNA or slow its translation into protein. Either mechanism can produce a similar protein level under constant input. Three pulses expose differences in how the protein level rises and falls.

Based on the gene-expression models in Ryzowicz & Yildirim (2023).

Which gene influences which?

On two simulated benchmarks, a linear model ranked known connections most accurately on average after each gene’s expression was centered and scaled.

For connections known to run in just one direction, the model gave that direction the higher score 86% of the time on DREAM4 and 53% on BoolODE, averaging over datasets. On BoolODE, even fitting the observations in reverse order left much of the overall ranking score intact.

Can variation within a class tell us what to keep?

A handwritten 3 can be sharp or blurry. If most 3s in the training set are blurry and most 5s and 8s are sharp, blur also looks like a difference between digits.

A rule that compared variation within and between the training classes gave extra weight to the selected directions. When blur stopped predicting the digit at test time, keeping that extra variation hurt accuracy.
